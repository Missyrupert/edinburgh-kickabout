# Privacy

- **No tracking.** There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, no cookies and no accounts.
- **Saves stay on your device.** Progress, pocket money, unlocks and settings are stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of them. Settings → Reset progress clears gameplay progress and pocket money only; your settings and any backup (`-bak`) or damaged-save (`-corrupt-`) copies stay until you clear site data.
- **Hosting.** The game is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and request details for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

# v2.5 Grassmarket + Castle: real vs fictional
## Real (references, stylised, not surveyed)
- Grassmarket, Old Town: tall rubble/ashlar sandstone tenements, crow-stepped gables, granite setts roadway, flagstone pavements, pubs and small shops at street level.
- West Bow / Victoria Street: the street bends and climbs at the east end; Victoria Street's brightly painted shopfronts.
- Covenanters' Memorial: a circular railed enclosure at the east end of the Grassmarket. Ours is a generic circular wall + railings + plain "IN MEMORY" stone, not a copy.
- Edinburgh Castle on its rock above the Grassmarket (south face; crag-and-tail, Half Moon Battery, Great Hall/Palace massing), stylised low-poly.
- Everyday: Sheffield bike stands, A-boards, barrels outside pubs, hanging baskets, wheelie bins.
## Fictional
- Every shop/pub name: The Last Drap, Fleming & Daughter, The Bow Barber, Books & Bawbees, The West Port Fish Bar, The Corn Exchange Cafe, Mackay's Bakery, The Half Moon, Paper & Press, Bow Cheese Co., Candlemaker, The Map Room, Brush & Brogue, Old Town Records, Hook & Line, Pure Dead Brilliant. Names that echo real places (West Port, Corn Exchange, Half Moon, Bow) are used as generic local words, not real businesses.
- Kicker's Close (and "ANNO 1687"), the street layout and building heights.
- Removed as stereotypes in v2.5: Tartan Tat & Tablet, Haggis Emporium, Wee Wizard Wands, Witchery Wools, The Sporran Shop, Kilts-R-Us, Cheese & Chuckles, Irn Age Cafe, Tablet & Tea.

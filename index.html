Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump
  • Mouse look
  • Hold & release left click to kick (aim up to chip it)
  • R fetch nearest ball · Esc pause

Touch

  • Left thumb joystick to walk (push far to run)
  • Drag right side to look
  • Hold KICK, release to shoot
  • BALL fetches the ball · JUMP

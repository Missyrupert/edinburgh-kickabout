# v2.1 The Pilton Parade: references and licensing

## Real (geography/architecture we draw on, not copied buildings)
- West Pilton, north Edinburgh: 1930s-50s Edinburgh Corporation housing (City Architect E. J. MacRae era): harled, pitched-roof three-storey flatted blocks, four-in-a-block cottage flats, drying greens, lock-up garage rows.
- The parade type: flat-roofed 1960s/70s shopping parades with flats above, found across the Pilton / Muirhouse / Granton schemes: single-storey shop units projecting under a canopy, flats set back above with a railed deck, roller-shutter boxes over windows, tiled stall risers, cast-iron drainpipes, extractor flues on the chippy.
- Views: the Firth of Forth, Fife hills, the Granton gasholder guide frame, tower blocks to the south-east.
- Everyday details: "salt & sauce" (the Edinburgh chippy sauce), gas/electric top-up cards in the newsagent, bookies' frosted windows, fly-posters on dead shutters.

## Fictional (invented for the game)
- The street layout and the parade itself (no single real parade is modelled).
- Shop names: The Pilton Plaice, The Wee Shop, Lucky Tattie, Curl Up & Dye, Video Nasty. Notices (Residents' Association, Lost Cat "Smokey", car boot sale, bingo, "0131 000 0000" phone numbers) and prices are made up.
- All characters (Mrs Kerr, the wee boy who fetches the ball, Jimmy).

## Tone
- Toned down: "DOGS MUST BE KEPT ON A LEAD. AND SO MUST WEANS." is now just "DOGS MUST BE KEPT ON A LEAD"; "NAE WASHING ON A SUNDAY" is now "DRYING GREEN · RESIDENTS ONLY"; "NAE TICK" and "(SINCE 1987)" went in v2.0.1->2.1 parade rebuild. Gameplay toasts unchanged.
